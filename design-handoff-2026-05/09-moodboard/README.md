## Forbidden refs

- **LinkedIn** — explicit anti-pattern in `BRAND.md`. Do not include.
- **Generic SaaS dashboards** (Notion clones, Slack-clones).
- Anything with **Tailwind blue chrome** as primary brand expression.

## What to look for in every ref

1. Surface variant variety — do they nest cards or differentiate?
2. Empty-state treatment — do they ship illustrations + actions, or a bare line?
3. Typography pairing — display vs body weights, mixed Arabic+Latin handling.
4. Single-accent discipline — do they have 1 accent CTA per screen, or a confetti of CTAs?
5. Whitespace as hierarchy — do dense lists breathe, or feel cramped?
