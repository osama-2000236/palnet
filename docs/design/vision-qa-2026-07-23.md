# Vision QA — web pass (2026-07-23)

Evidence: 232 full-page screenshots (29 routes × {ar-PS, en} × {light, dark} ×
{1440, 390}) captured with `apps/web/e2e/shots.mjs` against the local stack,
demo account, DB purged of QA fixtures first. Scored on the rubric from
[screen-critique-2026-07.md](./screen-critique-2026-07.md) — philosophy /
hierarchy / detail / functionality / restraint, ship gate ≥7.

Re-run: `node e2e/shots.mjs [--only=feed,saved] [--locale=] [--theme=] [--viewport=]`
from `apps/web`. It waits for `.animate-pulse` to clear — skeletons outlive
`networkidle` and blank shots are the failure mode.

## Findings and fixes

| #   | Surface(s)                                                          | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Fix                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | feed, activity, karama, premium, employer billing, search, messages | **Latin digits on Arabic surfaces.** next-intl formats numbers only for ICU `{x, number}` / plural `#`; a simple `{x}` placeholder is stringified as-is. Raw JSX renders (`{count}`, `String(count)`) had the same leak. | 16 message strings typed `{x, number}`; `formatNumber(value, locale)` at every raw render (activity tiles, employer KPIs, karama balance/costs/deltas, endorsement counts); `formatBadge` takes a `formatCount` the app supplies. |

## Verified after fix (re-shot, same harness)

`/saved` (both bookmark types, localized meta), `/activity` (٣ ١ ٠),
`/me/premium` (`تُحتسب ٥ دولارًا`), `/messages` (punctuation at the correct
end), `/search` (connect + `@handle`), feed @390 (icon-only actions,
`٣ من ٥`).

## Not changed (needs a product call, not a fix)

- `/me/premium` persuasion layer and multi-operator scan-speed layout — still
  the lead-gated Pass 2 items from [design-upgrade](./screen-critique-2026-07.md).
- Mobile posts expose 3 actions vs web's 5 (no repost/share) — parity gap, not
  a design defect. Belongs in [PARITY.md](./PARITY.md).
- List surfaces (network, saved, jobs) render every row as `card`. DESIGN.md
  §5.6 prefers `flat` container + `row` items; changing it is a visual-direction
  decision across four screens.
