# 9. WS-05 — Groups and events

## 9.1 What the market forces

§2.7: movement between governorates is constrained and Gaza is separated from the West Bank. Professional community here is therefore **either hyper-local or fully online**, with very little in between. §2.6: the نقابات already are the professional communities — a platform that ignores them competes with them and loses; one that hosts them gains their membership. §2.1: the diaspora's strongest pull is to a place, not to an industry.

**DECIDED — four seeded group archetypes**, because an empty Groups tab is worse than no Groups tab: **governorate** (one per the 16), **syndicate/professional body** (one per `PS_PROFESSIONAL_BODIES` entry), **university alumni** (one per `PS_UNIVERSITIES` entry), and **occupation family** (one per `PS_OCCUPATION_FAMILIES` entry). These are created by a seed migration, owned by the platform, and moderated by volunteers the owner appoints. Member-created groups are allowed from day one but the seeded set is what makes the tab non-empty on launch.

## 9.3 Rules — decided

- **A `PRIVATE` group's posts never enter the ranked feed**, not even for members. They are readable only inside the group route. This keeps the feed's privacy model to one rule rather than a per-post exception matrix.
- **A `PUBLIC` group's posts enter the feed for members only**, with `affinity = 0.5` in the §8.2 score.
- **Joining a seeded group is one tap and needs no approval** (`joinPolicy = OPEN`) except for `BODY` groups, which are `REQUEST` and are auto-approved when the member holds a `VERIFIED` `Licence` from that body. That auto-approval is the concrete payoff for §5's licence verification and it is why the two workstreams ship in adjacent phases.
- **`ALUMNI` groups auto-approve** on an `EDU_EMAIL` verification matching the university's domain.
- **Event `joinUrl` is never in a list DTO.** It is returned only by `GET /events/:slug` and only when the caller's `EventAttendee.state = GOING`. Otherwise a scraped list becomes a Zoom-bombing directory.
- **No in-person event may be created without a `governorateKey`.** A street address without a governorate cannot be reachability-checked, and §2.7 says reachability is the question.

## 9.5 Web, mobile, i18n, tests

**Web:** `groups`, `groups/[slug]`, `groups/[slug]/members`, `groups/[slug]/manage`, `groups/discover`, `events`, `events/[slug]`, `events/new`, `events/discover`. **Mobile:** the same nine as Expo Router files.

# 10. WS-06 — Hiring, end to end

## 10.1 What the market forces

This is the workstream the whole product turns on. §2.2: ~280,000 unemployed in the West Bank against a thin employer base means **the employer's attention is the scarce resource**, and 41.3% graduate unemployment means CVs do not discriminate. §2.7: reachability is a hard filter, not a preference. §2.8: the advance-fee scam is the existential threat. §2.4: wages are opaque, and the statutory floor is already modelled in `minimum-wage.ts`.

`docs/design/MATCHING.md` is an approved eight-section decision record with no implementation. `Application.matchSnapshot` is a committed column with no writer. `Job.screeningQuestions` is untyped JSON with no renderer. `Job.companyId` is nullable with no composer that uses it. `CompanyMember` has full RBAC and zero UI. `payBasis` has copy in four catalogs and no display.

**Every one of those is a promise the schema made. §10 keeps all of them.**

## 10.3 The match score — decided, both directions

`MATCHING.md` §1 says one model, two directions. **DECIDED, and this is the complete definition.**

```
matchScore(job, candidate) ∈ [0, 100]

MUST requirements are a gate, not a score:
  every MUST requirement the candidate fails → matchScore is still computed,
  but `mustFailures[]` is populated and the UI shows it.
  NO SCORE MAY AUTO-REJECT — MATCHING.md §5. The employer sees the failure and decides.

base = 100 * ( Σ_satisfied(w_i) / Σ_all(w_i) )   over MUST ∪ NICE, weight w_i

modifiers, applied multiplicatively, each clamped to [0.6, 1.15]:
  reachability   1.15 same governorate · 1.05 adjacent · 1.00 same region
                 0.85 cross-region · 0.60 requires permit  (REMOTE jobs → 1.00 always)
  evidence       0.90 + 0.25 * (candidate.evidenceScore / 100)
  availability   1.00 if availableFrom <= job.startsAt, else 0.85
  pay            1.00 if the candidate's expectedPay falls inside the posted range,
                 0.90 if above the top, 1.00 if the job hides its range
```

**Satisfaction by `RequirementKind`:**

| Kind | Satisfied when |
| --- | --- |
| `OCCUPATION` | The candidate holds an `OccupationClaim` on that key, **or** on a key in the same `Family` (0.7 credit) |
| `SKILL` | A canonical `ProfileSkill` match after folding (§5.2.3) |
| `LICENCE` | A `Licence` with `status = VERIFIED` on the required `bodyKey`. A `DECLARED` licence gives **0 credit**, because §2.6 |
| `STANDING` | `Standing.value >= intValue` on the required occupation |
| `EXPERIENCE_YEARS` | Summed `Experience` durations on matching occupation ≥ `intValue` |
| `LANGUAGE` | `ProfileLanguage.proficiency >= stringValue` on that language |
| `EDUCATION` | An `Education` row at or above the required level |
| `LOCATION` | `reachabilityBand ∈ {same, adjacent}` or the job is `REMOTE` |
| `AVAILABILITY` | `Profile.acceptingWork` or `openToWork` is true |

**The reverse direction** (`fitScore(candidate, job)`, shown to the seeker) uses the same terms with two changes: the pay modifier inverts (a job paying above the candidate's expectation scores higher), and a `belowMinimumWage(job)` hit caps the score at **40** and renders the existing never-pay warning. That reuse of `minimum-wage.ts` is deliberate — one implementation of the statutory floor, used by both sides.

**Fairness rules — MATCHING.md §5, restated as testable invariants:**

1. No score may auto-reject. `ApplicationStatus` may only move to `REJECTED` through a human action carrying a `RejectionReason`. Asserted in `application.service.spec.ts`.
2. Protected attributes are never inputs. The scorer's input type is a closed TypeScript interface that does not contain `gender`, `addressGender`, `age`, birth date, marital status, `originGovernorate`, refugee status, or religion. A test asserts the interface's key set exactly.
3. `matchSnapshot` freezes the score **and the weights version** at submission, so a later profile edit or a weight change cannot rewrite a past decision. The column already exists and says exactly this in its comment.
4. Every score is explainable: `matchSnapshot.terms[]` carries each requirement, whether it was satisfied, and its weight.

## 10.4 Employer verification — the anti-scam gate

**DECIDED, and this is the single most important rule in the document for user safety:**

> A `Job` is visible to nobody but its author until the poster is verified.

Two verification paths:

- **Company.** Submit `registrationNumber` + a registration document. Manual review in `/moderation`. `verificationState: UNVERIFIED → PENDING → VERIFIED | REJECTED`.
- **Individual poster** (`Job.companyId IS NULL`, the نجّار who needs two rooms rewired). Requires `Verification(PHONE, VERIFIED)` **and** at least one `CONFIRMED` `WorkProof` **or** one `VERIFIED` identity method. No document, no manual review — otherwise the individual-poster feature dies of friction, which would defeat the whole point of making `companyId` nullable.

**Additional hard rules:**

- A `SUSPENDED` employer's jobs are hidden immediately and their existing applicants are notified with a neutral, non-defamatory string.
- Three upheld `FEE_REQUEST` reports against one employer auto-suspends it pending review. The threshold is a named constant, `FEE_REPORT_SUSPEND_THRESHOLD = 3`, in `@baydar/shared`.
- The never-pay banner renders on **every** job detail, public job page, and the first message from any employer the viewer has not previously messaged. It is not dismissible. Copy is fixed in the catalog under `safety.neverPay` and the i18n gate treats it as a protected key that may not be deleted.

## 10.6 Salary insight — the k-anonymity rule

**DECIDED:** `GET /jobs/:id/salary-insight` returns a range only when **≥ 7 `WageObservation` rows** exist for the same `(occupationKey, governorateKey, payBasis, currency)` within 18 months. Below that, it widens to the governorate's region, then to national; if national is still under 7, it returns `null` and the UI says so plainly rather than inventing a number. Output is p25/p50/p75 rounded to the nearest 50 ILS, never a mean and never an exact figure, because an exact figure from a thin sample identifies the employer.

Observations are written on: a job posting with a disclosed range (`sourceKind = JOB_POSTING`, one row at the midpoint), and an `Application` reaching `HIRED` with `expectedPay` set (`CONFIRMED_HIRE`). Confirmed hires are weighted double at read time. No personally identifying column is stored on the observation — not the job id, not the user id. That is deliberate and it means the table cannot be joined back to a person.
