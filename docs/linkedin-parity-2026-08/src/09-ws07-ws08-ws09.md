# 11. WS-07 — Services and the freelance economy

## 11.1 What the market forces

§2.5 is the strongest commercial signal in the dataset: **10,000+ freelancers, 56% of IT/BPS activity is export, ICT is 5–7% of GDP.** These people already earn hard currency and already have a reputation problem — they are invisible to a local client and legible to an international one only through Upwork-shaped platforms that take a cut and do not know what a نقابة is.

`Profile.acceptingWork`, `servesAtClientSite` and `hasWorkshop` already exist with **no surface at all**. `CompanyKind` already has `SOLO`, `WORKSHOP`, `SHOP`, `FIRM`.

**Rule 2 (§4.2) is the boundary and it is absolute:** listings and inquiries, no money movement, no escrow, no ratings-for-hire economy separate from the one that already exists. Baydar is where the work is found and the evidence lives.

## 11.2 Data

## 11.3 Rules — decided

- **An inquiry opens a `ChatRoom`** rather than inventing a second messaging surface. `ServiceInquiry` carries the structure; the conversation happens where every other conversation happens.
- **Inquiries expire after 14 days** with no response, moving to `EXPIRED` and notifying the client. A silent inquiry is the services equivalent of the silent job rejection §1.3.2 already refuses to tolerate.
- **A provider may hold at most 20 active listings.** Above that it is a directory, not a profile.
- **A completed inquiry offers to create a `WorkProof`**, pre-filled, with the client as counterparty. This is the loop that makes the services surface feed the evidence spine rather than sitting beside it — and it is why WS-07 depends on WS-01.
- **No listing may state a price below the statutory floor** where the pricing model maps to one: `HOURLY` → 10.5 ILS, `DAILY` → 85 ILS. Reuses `minimumWageFloor()`. Rejected at write with `BELOW_MINIMUM_WAGE` and an explanatory copy string.
- **`QUOTE_ONLY` is the default** for `CRAFT`-track occupations, because a tiler who publishes a fixed price is competing on the one axis that destroys craft margins, and because most craft work genuinely cannot be priced without seeing it.

## 11.4 API, surfaces, tests

`POST/PATCH/DELETE /services` · `GET /services/:slug` · `GET /services/me` · `GET /services/search` (`?occupationKey=&governorateKey=&remote=&priceMax=&standingMin=`) · `POST /services/:slug/inquiries` · `GET /inquiries/me` (`?role=client|provider`) · `POST /inquiries/:id/respond` · `POST /inquiries/:id/close` · `POST /inquiries/:id/work-proof` · `POST /admin/internal/inquiries/expire`.

**Web:** `services`, `services/[slug]`, `services/search`, `services/manage`, `services/new`, `inquiries`. **Mobile:** the same six.

**New shared components:** `ServiceCard`, `ServiceHeader`, `PricingRow`, `InquiryForm`, `InquiryCard`, `CoverageMap` (a governorate chip grid, not a real map — a tile map is a bandwidth cost with no informational gain over 16 named chips).

**i18n:** new namespaces `services`, `inquiries`; ~148 keys per catalog.

**Tests:** minimum-wage rejection per pricing model; 20-listing cap; expiry cron idempotency; the inquiry→`ChatRoom`→`WorkProof` loop end-to-end; that no route anywhere accepts a payment field on an inquiry (a schema test asserting the Zod body has no `amount`/`payment`/`escrow` key — this is Rule 2's mechanical enforcement).

# 12. WS-08 — Learning

## 12.1 What the market forces

§2.3 kills hosted video: a 10-minute 480p lesson is ~50 MB, which on 2G is about five hours. §2.2 says 41.3% of graduates are unemployed, so the demand for retraining is enormous and the ability to pay for it is near zero. §2.6 says the credentialing authorities already exist. §2.5 says the ICT sector's own upskilling need (25%+ of PITA members prioritising AI integration) is real and export-funded.

**DECIDED — the shape.** Baydar Learning is **text-and-audio micro-lessons in Arabic**, authored inside Baydar, organised into `Path`s, free at the point of use for individuals, and monetised only through institutional sponsorship (§13.7). No hosted video in phase 1. No AI career coach (§3.7 #121 — a wrong answer costs someone a job, and the Arabic quality bar is not met).

**Why author rather than license.** Licensing an English catalog and subtitling it is the obvious move and it is wrong here: the content that matters is Palestinian-specific — how the نقابة licensing exam works, how to price craft work, what a Palestinian employment contract must contain, how to invoice a European client from Ramallah. None of that exists in a licensable catalog. **OWNER-INPUT:** who authors the seed catalog. The code path is identical whether it is the owner, a partner (MoL TVET, PITA, a university), or contracted writers.

## 12.2 Data

## 12.3 Rules and surfaces

- **Completing every lesson in a path with `issuesCertificate` creates a `Certificate`** on the member's profile with `issuerKey = "baydar"` and `issuerName` = the sponsor's name when sponsored. It never creates a `Standing` and never a `Licence` — §2.6, and `check-naming.mjs` will catch an attempt.
- **Lessons are fully downloadable for offline reading.** The web app caches a whole path's markdown in the service worker on explicit "download" (§15); mobile writes it to `expo-file-system`. Audio is downloaded only on an explicit second tap, with the megabyte cost stated in the button label.
- **Audio budget:** 32 kbit/s mono Opus, 8-minute ceiling → 1.83 MiB (1.92 MB) per lesson. Stated in the UI. Rejected at upload above 2.5 MB.
- **A path may not exceed 24 lessons.** Longer is a curriculum, not a path.

**API:** `GET /learning/paths` (`?occupationKey=&level=`) · `GET /learning/paths/:slug` · `POST /learning/paths/:slug/enroll` · `POST /learning/lessons/:id/complete` · `GET /learning/me` · `GET /learning/paths/:slug/download` (a single JSON bundle of every lesson body, for the offline cache) · admin CRUD under `/admin/learning/*`.

**Web:** `learning`, `learning/[slug]`, `learning/[slug]/[ordinal]`, `learning/me`. **Mobile:** the same four. **New components:** `PathCard`, `LessonReader`, `LessonAudioPlayer`, `ProgressRail`, `DownloadForOfflineButton`, `CertificateAwardCard`.

**i18n:** namespace `learning`, ~112 keys.

**Tests:** certificate issuance exactly once per path per user; offline bundle completeness; audio size gate; markdown subset reuse (the same parser spec as §7.4, proving one implementation); enrollment idempotency.

# 13. WS-09 — Payments, pricing and monetisation

## 13.1 What the market forces

Four facts, each of which invalidates a default assumption:

1. **Apple has no Palestine storefront** (§2.4). A resident's Apple ID is Jordanian or Egyptian.
2. **LinkedIn Premium Career is 5.9% of a statutory monthly minimum wage** (§2.4). Ported prices are declines.
3. **Wallets are the real rail** — Jawwal Pay (first final PMA licence, May 2020) and PalPay/Mahfazati (Bank of Palestine subsidiary), both operating in Gaza with UNDP partnerships. The enum members and env keys exist in the repo; **no adapter does**.
4. **Half the money is abroad** (§2.1). Diaspora payers have cards and USD.

Plus the permanent rule from §4.2: **money may never buy rank.**

## 13.2 The pricing table — decided, in ILS first

All prices are derived from the statutory monthly minimum wage of **1,880 ILS**, stated as a percentage, and then rounded to a legible number. The percentage is the specification; the shekel figure is the rounding.

| `USER_PREMIUM` (rename → `MEMBER_PLUS`) | Individuals                   | 1.6% of min wage  | **30 / month**      | ~8    | See §13.3 for what it may and may not contain                                   |

**DECIDED — `MEMBER_PLUS` may never contain:** application boost, profile boost, ranking priority, "featured applicant", "top applicant" placement, or any visibility a non-payer lacks in front of an employer. §2.2 and Rule 1. `HANDOFF.md` gap #1 records that two such rewards existed, debited points and granted nothing; they were correctly withdrawn. **They do not come back.**

**DECIDED — what `MEMBER_PLUS` actually contains**, all of which are genuine utility that costs Baydar money to provide and none of which is rank:

- Who viewed your profile, with the occupation and governorate breakdown (aggregate; §5).
- Salary insight on every job (the free tier gets it on 3 jobs per week).
- Unlimited saved searches and job alerts (free tier: 3).
- A second-language profile (`ProfileTranslation`) — a diaspora-facing feature with a real cost.
- Offline path downloads in Learning without a cap (free: 2 concurrent).
- A larger document locker (10 vs 2).
- Application insight: how many applied, the distribution of the fit scores, where you sit. **Information about the market, not a position in it.**

## 13.3 The Apple problem — the exact rule

**DECIDED.**

- **iOS build:** no purchase flow, no price, no "upgrade" call to action, no link to a purchase page. The entitlement screen shows current state and the sentence «يمكنك إدارة اشتراكك من موقع بيدر» with **no tappable URL**. This is the shape Apple's guidelines permit for a multiplatform service and it is the only shape that survives review while the storefront problem exists.
- **Android build:** may link out to the web checkout. Google Play supports Palestine as a country and its external-offer rules are looser.
- **Web:** the only place money changes hands.
- **Enforcement:** `scripts/check-release-placeholders.mjs` gains a rule that fails the build if any file under `apps/mobile/` for the iOS variant contains a `billing.checkout*` i18n key, a price literal, or a `Linking.openURL` to the billing host. A platform-conditional constant `PURCHASE_LINK_ALLOWED = Platform.OS === "android"` is the single switch, and the gate asserts it is the only one.

## 13.4 The wallet adapters — the shape

One interface, three implementations, plus cash. Today `wallets.ts:24–26` declares labels and env-key names only.

```ts
export interface WalletProvider {
  readonly method: Extract<PaymentMethod, "JAWWALPAY" | "PALPAY" | "REFLECT">;
  isConfigured(): boolean;
  /** Creates a provider-side charge and returns what the client must do next. */
  initiate(input: {
    invoiceId: string;
    amountMinor: number; // in the currency's minor unit
    currency: "ILS" | "JOD" | "USD";
    payerPhoneE164?: string;
    returnUrl: string;
    idempotencyKey: string; // = invoiceId; a retry must never double-charge
  }): Promise<
    | { kind: "REDIRECT"; url: string }
    | { kind: "PUSH_TO_APP"; reference: string; expiresAt: Date }
    | { kind: "OTP_REQUIRED"; reference: string }
  >;
  /** Verifies a provider callback. Signature verification is mandatory. */
  verifyCallback(
    headers: Record<string, string>,
    rawBody: Buffer,
  ): Promise<{
    reference: string;
    status: "PAID" | "FAILED" | "PENDING";
    providerPaymentId: string;
    amountMinor: number;
    currency: string;
  }>;
  /** Polls when a callback never arrives — mandatory on a 2G market. */
  reconcile(reference: string): Promise<"PAID" | "FAILED" | "PENDING">;
}
```

**Non-negotiable implementation rules:**

- Every callback route is `@Public()` (it cannot carry a session) **and** signature-verified, and each must be added to `EXPECTED_PUBLIC_ROUTES` in the route-coverage spec with a comment saying why — exactly as `POST /billing/webhooks/hyperpay` already is.
- `idempotencyKey = invoiceId`. `HANDOFF.md` records that the Karama checkout could be charged twice; the invoice-keyed idempotency is what makes that structurally impossible here.
- A `reconcile` cron runs every 10 minutes over invoices `OPEN` with a wallet reference older than 5 minutes. On 2G, callbacks get lost; without reconciliation a paid user stays unpaid.
- **OWNER-INPUT:** merchant onboarding with Jawwal Pay, PalPay and Reflect. Until credentials exist, `isConfigured()` returns false, the method is hidden from the catalog, and `GET /billing/catalog` already handles this — `billing.service.spec.ts:119–125` shows the `configured: false` shape is already tested. **Build all three adapters anyway.** The unconfigured state is already a first-class, tested condition.

**Cash at agent — DECIDED.** Both Jawwal Pay and PalPay run physical agent networks, which is how someone without a bank account pays for anything. Baydar's implementation is deliberately thin: `POST /billing/invoices/:id/cash-reference` issues a short numeric reference and an expiry; the member pays at any agent quoting it; the provider's callback or the reconcile cron marks it paid. No new payment model — it is a wallet flow with a `PUSH_TO_APP`-shaped response whose `reference` is human-readable.

## 13.5 Currency — decided

- **`Plan.currency` and `Invoice.currency` defaults change from `"USD"` to `"ILS"`.** A migration rewrites no existing rows (there are no production rows pre-launch) but the default must be right or the first real invoice is wrong.
- Supported: **ILS, JOD, USD.** Not EUR — the diaspora's card processors settle USD fine and a fourth currency is three more rounding rules.
- **Every amount is stored in minor units as an integer.** `amountCents` already is; the field is renamed `amountMinor` in the DTO layer only (the column name stays to avoid a rename migration on a monetary column, with a comment explaining the divergence — ILS and JOD have different minor-unit exponents: ILS 2, JOD **3**). **The JOD exponent is 3, not 2.** A JOD amount stored as if it had two decimals is a 10× error, and it is the single most likely money bug in this workstream. `packages/shared/src/money.ts` (new) owns `MINOR_UNITS: Record<Currency, number>` and every conversion goes through it.
- **FX:** `FX_FEED_URL` already exists in `env.ts:40`. Rates are fetched hourly, cached, and **stamped onto the invoice at creation** (`Invoice.fxRate`, `Invoice.fxBase` — new columns). A price displayed in ILS and charged in USD must use the rate the user saw, not the rate at capture.

## 13.6 Promotions — outside the ranked list

Rule 1 says money may not buy rank. Promotions are therefore **not a ranking boost**; they are a distinct slot.

**Placement rules — DECIDED:**

- **At most one promoted item per feed slate of 120**, inserted at a fixed position (index 4), never scored, never re-ordered, and rendered in a visually distinct `tinted` surface with a permanent «ترويج» label that is not dismissible and not styled to blend in.
- **At most one promoted job per job-search page**, at the top, above the results and outside them, in the same `tinted` treatment.
- **No promoted content in messages, notifications, groups, events, learning, or search-for-people.** Ever.
- **Targeting is occupation and governorate only.** No behavioural targeting, no lookalikes, no third-party pixels, no cross-site identifiers. This is a privacy decision and a bandwidth decision at once: a third-party tracker is both a leak and a payload.
- The promotions assembler is `promotions.service.ts`, which **may not import any ranker**, asserted by `check-ranking-purity.mjs`.

## 13.7 Institutional sponsorship

The realistic revenue line for a market this size, and the one LinkedIn has no equivalent of. `INSTITUTION_LEARNING` sponsors a `Path`; the sponsor's name appears on every issued `Certificate` and on the path card. Sponsored paths carry a disclosure line, are never given ranking preference in the learning catalog (Rule 1 applies here too), and the sponsor receives aggregate, k-anonymous completion statistics only — never a learner list.
